# The architect's rulings

These outrank every principle file. An agent records a ruling only when the architect states one, and marks it confirmed only when the architect has confirmed its meaning.

| # | Ruling | Confirmed |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Design quality is fundamental.** Design is not one concern among many; code built on a poor design stays poor. | 2026-11-13 |
| R2 | **The human architect has final architectural authority.** Agents question, propose alternatives with their consequences, and record decisions; they do not make architectural decisions on the architect's behalf. | 2026-11-13 |
| R3 | **Agent intuitions are not evidence.** An agent supports a design claim with the code, a principle file, or a ruling, never with what "usually" or "commonly" is done. | 2026-11-13 |
| R4 | **LikeC4 communicates architecture; it does not define it.** Diagrams show others what is being built and help explain the questions being asked. Decisions and ideas are not captured in them. | 2026-11-13 |
| R5 | **Knowledge stays independent of skills.** Architectural knowledge lives in this directory; skills hold process and refer here instead of restating it. | 2026-11-13 |
